Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains what information kingbot (the “Bot”) receives, what it stores, and what your options are. The Bot is operated by Noctis Systems, an independent project based in Vermont, United States (“we”, “us”).

1. How the Bot works

The Bot uses Discord’s HTTP interactions model. Discord sends us a signed request only when a user explicitly invokes one of the Bot’s slash commands. The Bot does not maintain a persistent Gateway connection, which means it cannot and does not read general chat messages, direct messages, voice activity, or member lists.

2. Information we receive

When you invoke a command, Discord includes the following in the interaction payload:

  • Your Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar hash
  • Your server nickname, roles, and permissions in that server
  • The server (guild) ID and channel ID where the command was used
  • The command name and any options you supplied, including text you typed and users you selected
  • An interaction ID and timestamp

We receive this data because Discord sends it as part of the request. We do not request it separately, and we do not use Privileged Gateway Intents.

3. Information we store

We do not store any of the information described above. Interaction payloads are processed in memory to generate a response and are then discarded. No database records are created.

Transient operational logs may briefly exist in our hosting provider’s systems for error diagnosis. These are not retained by us as a durable record.

4. Why we process this data

We process this data to operate the Bot’s features, to respond to your commands, to prevent abuse, and to fix errors. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or sale.

5. Where data is processed

The Bot runs on Cloudflare Workers, which processes requests at edge locations worldwide. Persistent data, where applicable, is stored in Cloudflare D1. Cloudflare acts as a data processor on our behalf and is subject to its own privacy commitments.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We do not share it with third parties except:

  • Discord, which necessarily receives the responses the Bot sends
  • Cloudflare, as our hosting and storage provider
  • Where required by law

7. Your choices and rights

  • Stop sharing data: stop using the Bot’s commands, or remove the Bot from your server.
  • Deletion: you can request deletion of any stored data associated with your Discord user ID or your server by contacting us at contact@noctis-systems.com. Include your Discord user ID or server ID so we can identify the records. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Access: you can request a copy of any stored data associated with you using the same contact method.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to object to processing or to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

8. Children

The Bot is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country. We do not knowingly collect data from such users. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data, including Discord request signature verification and storing credentials as encrypted secrets rather than in source code. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Material changes will be noted in the Bot’s GitHub repository when necessary.

11. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent to contact@noctis-systems.com.
